Detailed walkthrough

Use the app step by step, from market data to report

01

Search for a ticker and load market data

Start in the chart screen. Type a stock symbol, choose the market, and run the search. After the data loads, confirm that Current Price, Close, and the candlestick chart are populated.

  • Use the latest close as the current price when you want a fresh plan.
  • Switch the timeframe before interpreting the chart if your strategy depends on daily or longer candles.
  • If the symbol does not load, verify the market selection and ticker spelling.
02

Set the swing high, swing low, and direction

Fibonacci levels are only as useful as the price range you choose. Use a clear recent swing high and swing low from the timeframe you are analyzing.

Pullback formula Level Price = High - (High - Low) × Retracement% For rebound analysis, the app measures upward from the low: Low + (High - Low) × Retracement%.
  • Choose Pullback when planning entries after a move down from a high.
  • Choose Rebound when planning levels after a move up from a low.
03

Read the chart and Fibonacci Levels list together

The chart shows price movement, Fibonacci bands, and the current price line. The Fibonacci Levels list gives the exact level prices and the distance from the live price.

  • The Current Price row is highlighted in purple so it is easy to identify.
  • Distances help you see which staged entries are closest to triggering.
  • Use the chart for context and the list for exact price values.
04

Build the staged trade plan

Enter the investment amount, choose the first Fibonacci level where buying should start, and review the recommended shares for each stage.

Recommended shares Per-stage budget = Investment ÷ Number of selected stages Shares = floor(Per-stage budget ÷ Stage price) Remaining cash is assigned to the cheapest selected stages when possible.
  • Planned Cost shows how much cash the staged plan expects to use.
  • Target Profit estimates profit from staged entries to your target price.
  • Loss Check estimates downside if earlier stages are underwater at a later stage price.
05

Save records and export a PDF report

When the plan looks right, save it to Records. You can revisit saved setups later, compare them, or export a PDF report for your notes.

  • Use Save to store the current setup inside the app.
  • Use Save PDF when you want a clean summary of one strategy.
  • Use Print when you need a paper copy or a system print workflow.
