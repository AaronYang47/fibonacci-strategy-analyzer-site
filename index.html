What it helps with

A focused workspace for Fibonacci-based trade planning

Fibonacci Levels

Compare the current market price against key retracement levels and quickly see how far each stage is from the live price.

Staged Entry Plan

Split your investment amount across selected Fibonacci stages and review estimated shares, cost, target profit, and downside checks.

Market Data Refresh

Search a ticker, load recent candles, and use the latest close as the current price for a more practical plan.

Records and Reports

Save strategy setups for later review, then export or print PDF reports for your own notes or discussions.

How to use it

From ticker to trade plan in a few steps

  1. 01

    Search for a stock

    Enter a ticker symbol, choose the market, and load recent market data to fill the current price and chart context.

  2. 02

    Set the swing high and low

    Use the price range that matches your analysis. The app calculates Fibonacci levels from that range.

  3. 03

    Review the chart and levels

    Use the candlestick chart and Fibonacci Levels list to understand where the current price sits relative to each retracement.

  4. 04

    Build a staged entry plan

    Choose where buying should start, enter your investment amount, and adjust shares per stage if needed.

  5. 05

    Save, export, or print

    Save setups to Records, then generate a PDF report when you want a clean summary of the plan.
